About Little Sprouts

More Than Childcare.

Hi, I’m Jacqueline, Owner / Director of Little Sprouts Playschool in Elgin, TX. I created Little Sprouts to be more than childcare.

I wanted a warm, structured, small group environment where children feel safe, loved and encouraged to explore.

Little ones get to be little here. They will have 13 years of heavily structured education soon enough.

I serve families in Elgin, TX and surrounding areas, with full and part time childcare, preschool learning, a structured daily schedule and healthy home cooked meals, all in a safe, nurturing home environment. Children learn through guided play, social interaction, hands on activities and daily routines that build confidence and independence.

Spots are limited to maintain personalized care. Join our waitlist or schedule a visit by calling or sending a text.
